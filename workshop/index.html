AI Literacy Workshop · Intro to Physical AI

Intro to Physical AI: train a car to recognize what it sees.

A 15-minute intro lecture on Physical AI and how machines learn, a 15-minute lab tutorial with a live road-sign training demo, then ~60 minutes of hands-on lab: each team of three builds and deploys its own road-sign recognizer (left arrow · right arrow · stop) on the ESP32-P4 smart car, closing with a 30-minute demo & sharing session. No machine-learning or programming background required.

15-min lecture 15-min tutorial 60-min lab 30-min demo & sharing ESP32-P4 + camera No coding
