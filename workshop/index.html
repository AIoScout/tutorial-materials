Train a neural network on your laptop, run it on a microcontroller.

A 45-minute lesson on recognizing road signs with TensorFlow Lite Micro on the ESP32-P4, followed by a 90-minute project: each team builds and deploys a handwritten digit recognizer (0–9). No machine-learning or programming background required.
